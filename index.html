About Me

I'm Aarav Batra, a dedicated student-athlete pursuing a walk-on opportunity with Rutgers Football. As a Quarterback and Long Snapper, I bring versatility, coachability, and an unrelenting work ethic to the field every single day.

I've spent years training to compete at the collegiate level — pushing my limits in the weight room, studying film, and refining my mechanics. I believe in earning every rep, and I'm ready to prove myself against the best competition in the Big Ten.

Off the field, I'm committed to my academics and to being a positive presence in the locker room. I understand that being a walk-on means outworking everyone, and that's exactly what I plan to do.
